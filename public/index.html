From catalog to project.

  1. Browse the catalog and add parts to your cart.

  3. Choose a local meetup, Maxim delivery, or LBC shipping within the Philippines. LBC rates vary by destination and parcel details; the buyer pays the separately confirmed fee.
  4. Apply your promo code in the cart before placing the order.
  5. Save your private tracking link. Your order starts as Pending.
  6. Use the same verified CircuitByte account for your order history and Project Studio.
  7. Choose cash at handover or pay with QR Ph once your order is confirmed.
